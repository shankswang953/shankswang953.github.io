I am Jingfeng Wang, a postdoctoral research scholar at North Carolina State University. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Macau in 2024, and my B.S. from Sichuan University in 2020. My research focuses on optimal transport, numerical methods for partial differential equations, and machine learning. Discussions and collaborations are warmly welcomed.

## Research
{: #research}

I develop computational methods for biological data analysis, PDE-based simulation and optimization, and problems with optimal transport structure. The pages below introduce my research and related software.
